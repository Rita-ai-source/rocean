INSPECTION EQUIPMENT

Metrology-Grade Inspection Equipment

The ISO-certified shops in our network operate metrology-grade labs — and we verify every critical dimension and material property ourselves, on-site, before anything ships.

CMM — Coordinate Measuring Machine

The 3D coordinate measuring machines in our partner network provide precise dimensional verification of complex geometries with sub-micron repeatability. We use them for first article inspection and statistical sampling on production runs.

Optical Profile Projectors

Profile projectors provide magnified 2D measurement for complex contours and cross-sections. Essential for inspecting small features that are difficult to access with contact probes.

Digital Calipers & Micrometers

Calibrated digital measurement tools are used for rapid in-process inspection. Our QC engineers require routine checks throughout each run to catch deviations early — and we audit that the checks actually happened.

Custom Go/No-Go Gauges

For high-volume production, we specify custom go/no-go gauges so critical features can be checked fast and reliably at every station on the shop floor.

Material Testing Equipment

Spectrometry and mechanical testing instruments verify incoming raw material chemistry and physical properties. We provide material certificates with full heat and lot traceability.

Surface Roughness Measurement

Contact profilometer measures surface finish (Ra) to ensure machined and molded surfaces meet drawing callouts for function, sealing, or cosmetic appearance requirements.
