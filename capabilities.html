Scientific Molding — Process Development by Study

Every new mold is developed through a sequence of documented process studies — not trial-and-error. Each parameter is validated before full-rate production, so you get a process that is robust, repeatable, and transferable between machines and material lots.

Relative viscosity curve & injection speed optimization
Cavity balance verification (target <5% variation across cavities)
Pressure drop study across runner, gate & part
Process window study — robust temperature / pressure envelope
Gate freeze study — optimal holding time
Cooling time study — dimensional stability
Back pressure study — shot-to-shot consistency
Part weight validation — target <1% shot-weight variability

METAL INJECTION MOLDING

Precision MIM — Shaping the Future of Metal Manufacturing

Metal Injection Molding (MIM) is an advanced powder metallurgy process that combines the design freedom of plastic injection molding with the strength and performance of metal. At RC Precision, we deliver components made from fine metal powders through the advanced MIM process at our partner plants — with exceptional accuracy, repeatability, and material density.

Our mission is simple: deliver precision, performance, and reliability in every component we source and deliver — whether it's a batch of 10 or 10,000. From feedstock preparation to sintering, our partner plants' end-to-end MIM process ensures consistent quality for the most demanding applications in automotive, aerospace, medical, and consumer electronics.

Feedstock Preparation — fine metal powders + specialized binders
Injection Molding — complex geometries with high repeatability
Debinding — binder removal while preserving structure
Sintering — achieving full density and mechanical properties
Secondary machining & surface finishing available
High-volume production & rapid prototyping supported

CNC Machined Surface Texture — Patterns Cut by the Tool

Beyond precision geometry, our CNC mills and lathes can produce decorative and functional surface textures directly on the part — the pattern is carved by the cutting tool, not by chemistry. This means hard, durable facets in the base metal (no coatings that can peel, no chemicals that attack adjacent surfaces, and full pattern control from CAD).

Common applications include decorative finishes on consumer products and luxury goods, functional grip texture on knobs and handles, glare reduction on instrument panels, and brand identity / logo relief milling on production parts.

Linear / straight-grain finish — fine parallel grooves
Cross-hatch & lattice (Clous de Paris / diamond) patterns
Concentric rings & radial sunburst (engine-turned look)
Knurl-style diamond grip pattern for knobs & handles
Wave & 3D relief patterns, custom logo / artwork relief
Toolpath-only process — no etching, no masking, no chemicals
