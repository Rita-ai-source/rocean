OUR STORY

From a Small Team to a Full-Service Sourcing Partner

RC Precision was built by engineers with 20+ years of hands-on experience inside Chinese tooling and molding shops. We are an engineering-led sourcing partner: we don't own a factory, and that's deliberate — our QC answers to you, not to a production schedule. We support clients across automotive, electronics, medical, and consumer goods industries.

Today, our vetted network of ISO-certified shops houses CNC machining centers, injection molding lines, and advanced inspection equipment — and we personally oversee every project placed with them. Every part is backed by our independent quality control and the expertise of engineers who have spent careers mastering their craft.
